**Description:**
Super Screenshot lives in your menu bar and captures anything on screen. You can:
- Grab an area, a window or the full screen.
- Annotate with arrows, text, blur, highlights and backgrounds.
- Pin screenshots above other windows.
- Copy text out of any image with on-device OCR.
- Record video or GIFs with optional microphone, system audio and camera overlay.
- Polish recordings in Studio Mode with automatic zoom and cursor effects.

A Quick Access overlay and a capture history keep recent shots one click away. Uploading to your own S3-compatible or HTTP endpoint is optional. Everything runs on your Mac.
